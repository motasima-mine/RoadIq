- Stop: {stop_name} ({stop_city})
- Parking availability: {parking_pct}%
- Shower wait: {shower_wait} min
- Food options: {food_available}
- Diesel price advantage: {price_advantage}

YOUR TASK:
Write a personalized journey plan for this driver. Address them by first name. Cover these points in order:
1. Fuel timing — when they need to stop based on remaining miles
2. Why this specific Pilot stop is the best choice (parking, fuel deal, food match)
3. Shower situation (wait time, whether to reserve)
4. Food recommendation based on their preference and what's available
5. Loyalty perk — what their tier earns them at this stop
6. Vehicle health note (only if health is medium or poor)

RULES:
- Keep it under 200 words
- Use short paragraphs, not bullet lists
- Sound like a helpful co-pilot, not a robot
- Never invent data — only reference what's provided above
- If shower is not needed, skip that section
- If vehicle health is "good", skip that section
- Mention the stop name naturally, don't repeat it excessively
